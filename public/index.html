Why this site and not a telehealth cost guide

Search for GLP-1 pricing and most of what ranks is published by companies that sell GLP-1 products or provider access. A page that recommends the cheapest option while selling one of the options is not a comparison, it is a funnel.

This site sells nothing. There are no telehealth links, no pharmaceutical affiliate links and no compounding-pharmacy links anywhere in it, and there is no revenue arrangement with any manufacturer or government program. What we publish instead is our work: every figure carries the source it came from, the type of that source, and the date we last checked it.

We also tell you when we do not know. Prices in this market have moved repeatedly, and a confident number that is two months old is worse than an honest gap. Where we cannot confirm a figure against a primary source, we say so and link you to the official page instead of guessing. Read the methodology.

Every pathway we track

  • LillyDirect Self Pay — Eli Lilly's direct-to-patient cash-pay pharmacy. Requires a valid prescription. Bypasses insurance entirely, so the amount paid does not count toward any deductible or out-of-pocket maximum.
  • NovoCare Pharmacy — Novo Nordisk's direct-to-patient cash-pay pharmacy. Requires a valid prescription. Bypasses insurance entirely.
  • TrumpRx — Federal direct-purchase platform launched under Most-Favored-Nation pricing agreements with manufacturers. Prices on this platform have moved repeatedly since launch.
  • Manufacturer savings card — A manufacturer copay card used alongside commercial insurance. Universally excludes beneficiaries of federal healthcare programs.
  • Medicare GLP-1 Bridge — A Medicare access pathway reported to provide a fixed monthly beneficiary cost for a covered GLP-1, subject to clinical eligibility criteria.
  • Medicare Part D — Standard Part D coverage where the plan covers the indication. Cost varies by plan, formulary tier and phase of the benefit.
  • Patient assistance program — Manufacturer-funded assistance for patients below an income threshold. Income-gated and application-based.
